Grove & Vale

Rest your phone on the top of your acoustic, speaker toward the sound hole, and play. Grove & Vale listens through the wood and sends warm reverb and echoes back into the body, so the guitar blooms like it's in a big room. No cables, no amp.

Audio plug-in · C++ / JUCE

String tuning & creep logger

During a nylon string quality investigation at D'Addario, I built an AU/VST3 plug-in that tracked tuning at high speed and logged how strings drifted over time. It ran alongside three physical test rigs I set up with R&D and Engineering to measure how accurately the material had been made.

Live performance · Logic Pro

Backing-track system for my wedding band

I build the multitrack stems our band plays with two to four nights a week, including spoken countdowns and song-section cues so the drummer and I always know where we are in the song.
